# FoodFirst — Food at McGill Guidebook

A communal, living guidebook of food resources at McGill University and in Montreal: an interactive map of free and affordable food locations, a written history of food initiatives at McGill, and a resource bank for students facing food insecurity — and for students who want to volunteer.

**Live site:** *(add GitHub Pages URL here once published)*

## What's inside

- **Map** — food banks and affordable meals / volunteer opportunities across Montreal, embedded from uMap (OpenStreetMap)
- **Introduction and Goals** — what food security, food justice, and food sovereignty mean on a university campus
- **Written History** — food initiatives and mobilization at McGill, from before 1999 to 2023
- **Resource Bank** — free meals (Midnight Kitchen, The People's Potato, SNAC) and affordable meals near campus
- **How to Start a Club/Initiative**, **Mobilization Efforts**, **Interesting Projects**, and **Works Cited**

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in a single file (`index.html`) — no build step. The map is an embedded uMap / OpenStreetMap map.

## About the project

FoodFirst was built so the food organizations already working at McGill could coordinate with each other instead of duplicating efforts — one shared platform where existing initiatives can be found, compared, and revived.
